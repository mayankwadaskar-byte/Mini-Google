How does this work? (and things to try)

Old way: count how many times the word appears in each page. Spammers just repeat the word again and again!

Surfer way: a pretend surfer starts on a random page and makes 100,000 moves. 15% of the time he gets bored and jumps to any random page. Otherwise he clicks a random link. Pages that lots of other pages link to get visited a lot, so they get a high surfer score. This idea is called PageRank, the idea that made Google famous.

  • Search exam or pizza and look for the red "Spam smell" tag.
  • Watch the cards slide in the right column: that is each page moving from its old rank to its surfer rank. The lines join the same page in both columns.
  • Hover a card: the same page lights up in the other column and on the map.
  • Press Run the surfer again. The scores wiggle a little, because the surfer is random.
  • Press Watch the surfer for a slow-motion walk (the real run does 100,000 moves in a blink).
  • Try Dark mode, 1998 mode and the sound button (it is off until you switch it on).
